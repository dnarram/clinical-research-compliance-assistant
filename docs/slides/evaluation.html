Before connecting the model I checked retrieval on its own. Twelve in-domain queries, in English and Spanish, and six out-of-domain ones. Spanish scores a little lower but finds the same passages. The two groups do not overlap, and the refusal threshold is the midpoint of the gap. Then the golden set: fifty runs. Retrieval was already perfect at document level, but reading every answer showed lists cut in half by chunking, a multi-step question refused outright and Spanish refusals written in English. Section expansion, document routing and prompt v4 took answer keyword coverage from 0.82 to 0.97 with identical scoring. What remains: a multi-hop reporting chain still misses one timeline, and routing cost one section hit in Spanish — I report it rather than tune against my own test set.
